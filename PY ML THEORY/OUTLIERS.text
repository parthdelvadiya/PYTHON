What are Outliers?
    Outliers = extreme values that are very different from other data

[10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 500]
500 --> Outlier

Why are Outliers a Problem? 
    Affect mean, variance
    Can reduce accuracy

How to Detect Outliers
    IQR Method (Most Important ⭐)

        Find Quartiles:

            Q1 = 25%
            Q3 = 75%

        Compute:
            IQR=Q3−Q1

        Define bounds:

            Lower = Q1 − 1.5 × IQR
            Upper = Q3 + 1.5 × IQR

Q1 = df['salary'].quantile(0.25)
Q3 = df['salary'].quantile(0.75)

IQR = Q3 - Q1

lower = Q1 - 1.5 * IQR
upper = Q3 + 1.5 * IQR

outliers = df[(df['salary'] < lower) | (df['salary'] > upper)]



salary = [10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 100]

✅ Step 1: Find Q1 (25%)
    Q1 = df['salary'].quantile(0.25)

Data is sorted:
[10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 100]
    25% position → between 1st & 2nd value
        👉 Q1 ≈ 12–13

✅ Step 2: Find Q3 (75%)
    Q3 = df['salary'].quantile(0.75)

    75% position → near upper side
        👉 Q3 ≈ 18–20

✅ Step 3: Compute IQR
    IQR = Q3 - Q1

👉 Example:
    Q3 = 20  
    Q1 = 12  
    IQR = 8

✅ Step 4: Define Bounds

    lower = Q1 - 1.5 * IQR
    upper = Q3 + 1.5 * IQR

👉 Why 1.5?
    It’s a statistical rule to detect extreme values

Calculate:
    lower = 12 - (1.5 × 8) = 12 - 12 = 0  
    upper = 20 + (1.5 × 8) = 20 + 12 = 32

✅ Step 5: Detect Outliers
    df[(df['salary'] < lower) | (df['salary'] > upper)]

👉 Meaning:

Check each value:
10 → OK  
12 → OK  
15 → OK  
18 → OK  
20 → OK  
100 → ❌ OUTLIER
