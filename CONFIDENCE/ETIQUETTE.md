# Professional Interview Etiquette & English Communication Guide

# Why Communication Matters

Many candidates have good technical skills but lose opportunities because of:

* Poor communication
* Interrupting interviewers
* Speaking too casually
* Lack of confidence
* Unprofessional behavior

Interviewers evaluate:

* Communication skills
* Confidence
* Professionalism
* Listening ability
* Teamwork attitude
* Learning mindset

---

# Before Joining the Interview

## Join Early

Always join:

```text
5-10 minutes before interview time.
```

Never join late without informing HR.

---

## Check Equipment

Verify:

```text
Microphone
Camera
Internet Connection
Headphones
Background Noise
```

---

## Dress Professionally

Recommended:

```text
Clean shirt
Well-groomed appearance
Simple background
```

Avoid:

```text
Sleeping clothes
Messy room
Eating during interview
```

---

# How To Start The Conversation

When interviewer joins:

```text
Good morning sir.

Good morning ma'am.

I hope you are doing well.
Thank you for taking the time to interview me today.
```

Or

```text
Hello.

Good afternoon.

Thank you for giving me this opportunity.
```

```text
Good morning.
```

# Voice Tone During Interview

Speak:

```text
Clearly
Slowly
Confidently
Politely
```

Avoid:

```text
Speaking too fast
Mumbling
Using slang
```

Examples:

Bad:

```text
Yeah
Yep
Nah
Bro
Okay okay
```

Good:

```text
Yes sir.
Certainly.
Absolutely.
I understand.
Thank you.
```

---

# How To Address Interviewers

Use:

```text
Sir
Ma'am
Mr. Name
Ms. Name
```

Examples:

```text
Yes sir.

Thank you sir.

That's a good question, sir.
```

---

# Listening Etiquette

Never interrupt.

Wait until interviewer finishes speaking.

Bad:

```text
No no I know this.
```

Good:

```text
Certainly.

May I answer?
```

---

# If You Did Not Hear The Question

Say:

```text
I apologize, could you please repeat the question?
```

Or

```text
I am sorry, the audio was unclear. Could you please repeat that?
```

Never say:

```text
What?
Huh?
Come again?
```

---

# If You Don't Know The Answer

Bad:

```text
No idea.
I don't know.
```

Good:

```text
I am not completely familiar with that concept.

However, based on my understanding...
```

Or

```text
I haven't worked on that particular topic yet, but I would be interested in learning it.
```

---

# How To Answer Technical Questions

Structure:

```text
Definition

Explanation

Example

Use Case
```

Example:

Question:

```text
What is Django?
```

Answer:

```text
Django is a high-level Python web framework.

It follows the MVT architecture and helps developers build web applications quickly.

It provides features such as authentication, ORM, admin panel, and security.

I have used Django in several projects to build REST APIs and web applications.
```

---

# If You Need Time To Think

Say:

```text
That's a good question.

Let me think about it for a moment.
```

This sounds professional.

---

# When Explaining Projects

Use this format:

```text
Project Name

Purpose

Technologies Used

My Role

Challenges

Outcome
```

Example:

```text
I developed an Employee Management System using Django and Django REST Framework.

The goal was to manage employee records and perform CRUD operations.

I implemented authentication, APIs, and database integration.

This project helped me improve my backend development skills.
```

# HR Round Etiquette

Questions HR May Ask:

```text
Tell me about yourself.

Why should we hire you?

What are your strengths?

What are your weaknesses?

Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

Why do you want to join our company?
```

Always:

```text
Stay positive.

Avoid criticizing previous companies.

Avoid discussing salary immediately.

Show willingness to learn.
```

---

# Talking About Salary

Bad:

```text
How much salary will I get?
```

Good:

```text
I am more focused on learning opportunities and career growth.

However, I would appreciate understanding the compensation structure for this role.
```

---

# Interview Body Language

## Maintain Eye Contact

Look at:

```text
Camera occasionally
Interviewer when speaking
```

Avoid:

```text
Looking away constantly
Looking at phone
```

---

## Posture

Good:

```text
Sit straight

Shoulders relaxed

Hands visible
```

Bad:

```text
Lying down

Leaning excessively

Moving constantly
```

---

# Video Call Etiquette

Before speaking:

```text
Unmute microphone
```

After speaking:

```text
Mute if necessary
```

Avoid:

```text
Background noise
Talking over interviewer
Using phone during interview
```

---

# Telephone Interview Etiquette

When answering call:

```text
Hello.

Good morning.

This is Parth Delvadiya speaking.
```

If HR calls:

```text
Hello.

Good afternoon.

Thank you for calling.
```

---

# During Phone Call

Speak slowly.

Smile while talking.

It naturally improves voice quality.

Keep answers:

```text
Clear
Short
Relevant
```

---

# Questions You Can Ask Interviewer

At the end:

```text
What would success look like in this role?

What technologies does the team currently use?

What learning opportunities are available?

What does a typical day look like for this position?
```

---

# Questions To Avoid

Avoid:

```text
How many leaves do I get?

How quickly can I get promoted?

How much overtime is there?

Can I work less?
```

During the first interview.

---

# Ending The Interview

Say:

```text
Thank you for your time.

It was a pleasure speaking with you.

I appreciate the opportunity to discuss my background and skills.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Have a great day.
```

---

# Golden Rules

## Always

```text
Be respectful.

Listen carefully.

Speak clearly.

Stay positive.

Maintain confidence.

Be honest.

Show willingness to learn.
```

## Never

```text
Interrupt interviewer.

Argue.

Use slang.

Lie.

Speak negatively about others.

Show frustration.
```

---

# One-Line Rule For Every Interview

```text
Be confident, be polite, listen carefully, and communicate clearly.
```
