A boat takes 4 hrs downstream and 6 hrs upstream for same distance. Find ratio of speeds.
Solution:
Let distance = D
Downstream speed = D/4
Upstream speed = D/6
Boat speed = (D/4 + D/6)/2 = (3D + 2D)/12 = 5D/12 ÷ 2 = 5D/24
Stream speed = (D/4 − D/6)/2 = (3D − 2D)/12 = D/12 ÷ 2 = D/24
👉 Ratio (Boat : Stream) = 5 : 1

A 40L mixture has milk & water in ratio 3:1.
10L is removed and replaced with water. Find final milk.
Initial milk = 30L
(3/4 * 40)
When you remove mixture, you remove milk and water in the same ratio (3:1)
So milk removed = 3/4 × 10 = 7.5 L
30 − 7.5 = 22.5 L
