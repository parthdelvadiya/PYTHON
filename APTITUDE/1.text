A boat takes 4 hrs downstream and 6 hrs upstream for same distance. Find ratio of speeds.
Solution:
Let distance = D
Downstream speed = D/4
Upstream speed = D/6
Boat speed = (D/4 + D/6)/2 = (3D + 2D)/12 = 5D/12 ÷ 2 = 5D/24
Stream speed = (D/4 − D/6)/2 = (3D − 2D)/12 = D/12 ÷ 2 = D/24
👉 Ratio (Boat : Stream) = 5 : 1

30 − 7.5 = 22.5 L

A can complete a work in 20 days and B in 30 days.
They work together for 6 days. Then A leaves.
In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?

A = 1/20 , B = 1/30
1/20 + 1/30 = 1/12 
1/12 * 6 = 1/2 
1 - 1/2 = 1/2 
1/2 / 1/30 = 15
